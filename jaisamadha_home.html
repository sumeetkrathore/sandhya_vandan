About Ashram

In the heart of Shikarpur, Sindh, Pakistan, stands Pooj Samadha Ashram, where bhagti and service have flowed together for nearly 250 years. This sacred ashram is more than just a historical landmark. It is a home for devotion, a center for community care, and a guardian of precious cultural traditions and Dharmic rituals. What makes it even more special is its location: built on parts of ancient cremation grounds, it reminds us that where life ends, divine grace begins.

Satguru Baba Narayan Bhajan Sahib

Born January 29, 1990 · Shikarpur

Hazoori Roop Suhina Satguru Baba Narayan Bhajan is the present Gadhinasheen of Pujiya Shree Samadha Ashram. Born to Dewan Narayan Das, he has been guiding the ashram and its devotees across Pakistan and India with love, devotion, and seva since 2008.
